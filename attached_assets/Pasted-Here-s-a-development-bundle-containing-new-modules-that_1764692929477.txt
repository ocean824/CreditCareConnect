Here's a development bundle containing new modules that extend your app to support Metro 2 files, add more FCRA compliance checks, draft legally grounded dispute letters with a generative‑AI layer, employ multiple extraction strategies, and include a UI component for manual review. Metro 2 support: Adds a parser (metro2Parser.ts) that invokes the open‑source Moov Metro 2 CLI to read, validate and parse Metro 2 files.
